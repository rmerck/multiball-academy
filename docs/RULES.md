# Rules

Multiball Academy is a flight academy on a pinball table. You have three balls to fly six sorties, earn rank, climb from FL100 to FL500, play three kinds of multiball, and, for the best pilots, earn your wings and fly the Air Show.

## The table

| Shot | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Left and right loop | The outer orbits, all the way round the arch | A loop is made when the ball goes round the top (or drops from the arch into an R-A-D lane). Flow, combos, jackpots; LOOP DRILL |
| Runway and Skyway | The two ramps, left and right of centre | Each carries the ball over the table to the opposite flipper. Flow, combos, jackpots; ramps relight the Mission and light the Flight Deck |
| Hangar | Left of centre, behind three bay doors | Locks balls for Squadron multiball; super jackpot; DOGFIGHT jackpot; the Air Show finale |
| Afterburner | The centre lane, under the Bandit drone | Spins, the boost meter, starts overdrive. The Bandit above it takes hits |
| Mission | The scoop right of centre (left flipper) | Starts sorties; hands out the extra ball and extra credit; starts Wings Ceremony and the Air Show |
| Flight Deck | Top right, with its own flipper (right button) | Reached by the Skyway when its diverter is lit. A-C-E stand-ups; the CAT SHOT rides the ball to the Hangar and locks it |
| R-A-D lanes | Three lanes at the top | Skill shot; all three light overdrive |
| Jets | Under the R-A-D lanes | Boost meter; choose the sortie on offer |
| Nest targets | Two, beside the jets | Boost meter; both light extra credit and NIGHT LANDING |
| G-FORCE | The captive ball, upper left | Each hit 1G (a hard one 2G). 3G lights DOGFIGHT; 5G pays 50,000 |
| T-A-X-I | Four stand-ups round the field | All four light FORMATION |
| F-L-A-P-S | Five rollovers in the middle of the field | All five relight the Mission and add bonus X |
| W-I-N-G lanes | The two inlanes and two outlanes | All four relight the kickback |
| Kickback | Left outlane | Lit at the start of a game: returns one ball to the left flipper |

A blinking arrow in front of a shot means "shoot this now"; a fast blink means it pays the most.

The flipper buttons also move the lit R-A-D lanes and the lit W-I-N-G letters one place left or right.

## Skill shot

Every ball you plunge yourself has a skill shot.

- One R-A-D lane blinks. The flipper buttons move it. Drop the ball through it: 25,000 times the ball number. Any other R-A-D lane: 5,000 times the ball number.
- Super skill shot: plunge hard enough that the ball laps the whole arch to the left flipper, then shoot the Skyway within 5 seconds: 75,000 times the ball number, and the Skyway takes the ball to the Flight Deck.

## Sorties and rank

Six sorties, each lit by its own kind of shot and started at the Mission. At the start of a game the Mission is lit and offers LOOP DRILL; the jets move the offer. Once a sortie is over, two ramp shots (three after the third) or F-L-A-P-S light the Mission again.

| Sortie | Lit by | Task | Clock | Each step | Pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LOOP DRILL | 3 loops | 3 loops; the other side from the last pays double | 40 s | 25,000 | 125,000 |
| RAMP RUNS | 4 ramps | 4 ramps, the lit one moving to the other side after each | 40 s | 20,000 | 125,000 |
| GUNNERY | 3 Bandit hits | 4 Bandit hits, each adding 5 seconds; BANDIT DOWN during it doubles the pass | 30 s | 15,000 | 100,000 (200,000) |
| FORMATION | T-A-X-I | The lit arrow 3 times; it moves on every 5 seconds | 45 s | 30,000 | 150,000 |
| NIGHT LANDING | both nest targets | The Runway (the approach), then the Skyway (the landing) | 40 s | 10,000 | a hurry-up from 300,000, 7,500 less each second, at least 100,000 |
| DOGFIGHT | 3G on G-FORCE | Two balls. A Bandit hit lights the Hangar: DOGFIGHT JACKPOT 100,000, twice | 45 s | 25,000 | 200,000 |

- A qualified sortie blinks its lamp and holds the Mission's offer; the Mission starts the qualified ones first. From the third sortie started in a round, each qualifier asks for twice as many.
- A sortie's clock stands still while no ball is free and through any multiball except DOGFIGHT's own. The pylon in the middle of the field rises during sorties: a hit adds 3 seconds.
- A sortie carried over a drain keeps its progress and at least 15 seconds. A tilt ends it.
- Each sortie won raises your rank (ROOKIE, TRAINEE, WINGMATE, PILOT, ACE, INSTRUCTOR) and your Altitude by one flight level.

## Altitude

The game climbs from FL100 to FL500, one level for each sortie won and to the top after the Air Show. It never comes down within a game. Each level makes the table busier and harder: the devices run faster (the Prop Wash spins at FL200, the Air Pocket pulls at FL300), the Bandit takes more hits to knock down (3, 4, 4, 5, 6), sortie clocks and the ball saver get shorter (the saver is 10 seconds at FL100 and 4 at FL500), F-L-A-P-S letters time out from FL300, and from FL300 every lock shuts the Hangar again. The lights move from day blue to night violet.

## Scramble multiball (two balls)

Jets, spins, Bandit hits, the nest targets and CAT SHOTs fill the boost meter (two CAT SHOTs fill the first one). Full: a second ball launches, `SCRAMBLE MULTIBALL`. The meter asks for more each time. The ramps are lit for jackpots, and the Flight Deck stays open. With three balls already on the table a full meter pays `SCRAMBLE BONUS` 25,000 instead.

## Squadron multiball (three balls)

Hit all three bay doors and the bank drops: the Hangar is open and `LOCK IS LIT`. A ball shot into it is locked in view (`BALL 1 LOCKED`) and you get a new ball. The first multiball of a game keeps the Hangar open for one more lock; later ones (and every lock from FL300) need the doors again. A CAT SHOT from the Flight Deck locks its ball whatever the doors say. With two locked, the third ball in starts `SQUADRON MULTIBALL`: all three are let go, with 15 seconds of grace.

Ramps and loops are lit for jackpots: 50,000, 75,000, 100,000, then 150,000. A pylon hit adds 25,000 to the next. Knock down the Bandit (or make three jackpots) and the Hangar is lit for the `SUPER JACKPOT`, 500,000. The second Squadron multiball of a game lights the extra ball.

## The Flight Deck

Two ramp shots light the Skyway's diverter (`FLIGHT DECK IS LIT`); the next Skyway takes the ball up to the deck. Use the deck flipper (right button) on A-C-E (5,000 a letter; all three 50,000, then 25,000 more each time) and the CAT SHOT (5,000, a lock and a boost). The deck drain returns the ball to the left inlane.

## Combos

Flow shots are the two loops, the two ramps and the Afterburner. A flow shot made within 4 seconds of a different flow shot is a combo: 20,000, 40,000, 80,000 and 150,000 for the second to fifth shot (`COMBO 2X` to `COMBO 5X`). The first five-shot chain of a game lights the extra ball.

## Overdrive

Light all three R-A-D lanes, then shoot the Afterburner: `OVERDRIVE 2X` doubles nearly every award for 20 seconds. Complete R-A-D again while it runs for 3X and a fresh 20 seconds.

## Extra credit and extra balls

- Extra credit: light both nest targets, then shoot the Mission. One of: 25,000, 50,000, kickback relit, bonus multiplier +1, 10 seconds of ball saver, boost meter +15, overdrive lit.
- Extra ball: lit at the Mission by the third sortie won, the first five-shot combo chain and the second Squadron multiball. At most two a game, one held at a time; otherwise 100,000.

## Saves

- Ball saver: a ball that drains within its first 10 seconds of play (less at higher Altitude) is given back.
- A ball that drains within 2.5 seconds of being returned by the Mission or the Hangar is given back.
- Kickback: lit at the start of the game; W-I-N-G relights it, or raises the bonus multiplier if it is lit.

## End-of-ball bonus

| For | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Each sortie won so far this game | 20,000 |
| Each ball locked in the Hangar | 10,000 |
| Each combo this ball | 5,000 |
| Each set completed this ball (R-A-D, W-I-N-G, F-L-A-P-S, T-A-X-I, A-C-E) | 5,000 |
| Each switch hit this ball, up to 400 | 250 |

The sum is multiplied by the bonus multiplier, 1X to 5X (W-I-N-G with the kickback lit, F-L-A-P-S, extra credit).

## Wings Ceremony

Fly all six sorties (won or not) and the Mission is lit for the `WINGS CEREMONY`: 60 seconds (less at higher Altitude) in which every major shot and every Bandit hit pays 100,000. Once a round.

## Air Show

Win all six sorties, play a Squadron and a Scramble multiball, and spell A-C-E: the Air Show is lit at the Mission (the sortie window says what is still missing).

1. `AIR SHOW`: every ball there is comes into play, three in all, with 30 seconds of grace. Nothing locks and no sortie runs.
2. `FORMATION PASS`: all seven major shots are lit. Each pays 100,000 and goes out.
3. `FINALE`: the Bandit comes back with nine armour. Knock it down, then shoot the Hangar: the `AIR SHOW SUPER JACKPOT`, 1,000,000, and the rank of CHANCELLOR.
4. Victory laps: every major shot pays 50,000 until one ball is left.

If it ends early, the shots made stay made and three ramps light it again. After a completed Air Show every sortie opens again with double pass awards.

## Nudge and tilt

X bumps the table from the left and sends the ball right, `.` does the opposite, and Up bumps from the bottom and sends the ball up the table.

Each nudge adds tilt heat, which cools over 2.5 seconds. Two quick nudges show a warning. Three inside about a second tilt the table: the flippers and plunger go dead, nothing scores, no ball is given back, the end-of-ball bonus is lost and a running sortie ends, until the last ball on the table has drained. Locked balls stay locked. The next ball starts clean.

The rules live in `src/core/Rules` and `src/core/Courses` (the sortie catalogue) with no dependency on physics, and are unit tested event by event.
