# Rules

Multiball Academy is a flight academy on a pinball table. You have three balls to pass courses, earn rank, play two kinds of multiball and, for the few who pass all six courses in one game, sit the final exam on Graduation Day.

## The table

| Shot | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Left and right circuit | The outer lanes, all the way round the arch | A circuit is made when the ball goes round the top (or drops from the arch into a roll call lane). Flow, combos, jackpots |
| Runway and Skyway | The two ramps, left and right of centre | Each carries the ball over the table to the opposite flipper. Flow, combos, jackpots; ramps relight the Briefing room |
| Hangar | Left of centre, behind three bay doors | Locks balls for Academy multiball; super jackpot |
| Briefing room | The scoop, dead centre | Starts courses; hands out the extra ball and extra credit; starts Graduation Day |
| Turbine | The spinner lane, right of centre | Spins, turbo meter, starts overdrive |
| Roll call lanes | Three lanes at the top | Skill shot; all three light overdrive |
| Thruster bumpers | Under the roll call lanes | Turbo meter; choose the course on offer |
| Turbo targets | Two, either side of the bumpers | Turbo meter; both light extra credit |
| W-I-N-G lanes | The two inlanes and two outlanes | All four relight the kickback |
| Kickback | Left outlane | Lit at the start of a game: returns one ball to the left flipper |

A blinking arrow in front of a shot means "shoot this now".

The flipper buttons also move the lit roll call lanes and the lit W-I-N-G letters one place left or right, so a lane you still need can be put under the ball.

## Skill shot

Every ball you plunge yourself has a skill shot.

- One roll call lane blinks. The flipper buttons move it. Drop the ball through it: 25,000 times the ball number. Any other roll call lane: 5,000 times the ball number.
- Super skill shot: plunge hard enough that the ball laps the whole arch to the left flipper, then shoot the Skyway within 5 seconds: 75,000 times the ball number.

## Courses and rank

The Briefing room is lit for a course at the start of the game. One course lamp blinks: that is the course on offer, and each thruster bumper hit moves the offer to the next course you have not passed. Shoot the Briefing room to start it (`CLASS IN SESSION`).

| Course | Time | Task | Each step pays | Passing pays |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BUMPER BASICS 101 | 35 s | 12 thruster bumper hits | 3,000 | 100,000 |
| RUNWAY DRILLS 103 | 35 s | 4 ramps, either one | 20,000 | 125,000 |
| CIRCUIT TRAINING 201 | 40 s | 2 circuits | 30,000 | 150,000 |
| HANGAR SAFETY 202 | 40 s | Hit all three bay doors, then enter the Hangar | 10,000 a door | 150,000 |
| TURBINE THEORY 301 | 35 s | 36 spins | the spins | 250,000 at the start, falling 5,000 a second to 100,000 |
| NAVIGATION 302 | 45 s | Make the lit shot 3 times; it moves on when made, or after 8 s | 30,000 | 200,000 |

- One course runs at a time. Its clock runs while a ball is free on the table; it stops while every ball is held (in the Briefing room, in the Hangar, on a ramp) or waiting on the plunger.
- Out of time: `COURSE FAILED`. Nothing is lost and the course can be taken again.
- If the ball drains, the course carries on with the next ball, with at least 15 seconds on the clock.
- After a course ends, two ramp shots light the Briefing room again (three after the third course of a game).
- In NAVIGATION the Hangar is skipped while its doors are up.

Rank: ROOKIE at the start, then TRAINEE, WINGMATE, PILOT, ACE and INSTRUCTOR for one to five courses passed. The sixth course lights Graduation Day. The top rank, CHANCELLOR, is earned there and nowhere else. Rank is never lost during a game and is saved with a high score.

## Turbo multiball (two balls)

The turbo meter counts thruster bumper hits (+1), spins (+1) and turbo targets (+5). It carries over from ball to ball. When it fills, at 60, a second ball is launched: `TURBO MULTIBALL`. After that it takes 200 more each time.

- Jackpots are lit on both ramps: 50,000, then 75,000, 100,000 and 150,000 from then on.
- For the first 10 seconds a ball that drains is launched again.
- It ends when one ball is left.
- If there is no room for another ball (three is the most, counting balls locked in the Hangar) a full meter pays `TURBO BONUS`, 25,000, instead.

## Academy multiball (three balls)

1. Hit all three bay doors. Each hit marks a door (2,000); marks stay from ball to ball. With all three marked the doors drop together: `LOCK IS LIT`.
2. Shoot the Hangar: `BALL 1 LOCKED`. The ball stays in its bay, in view, and if no other ball is in play you get a new ball to plunge, with a skill shot.
3. Lock a second ball the same way. For the first multiball of a game the doors stay down after the first lock; after the second they come back up and must be opened again.
4. A third ball into the Hangar starts `ACADEMY MULTIBALL`: all three balls are let go.

During it:

- Jackpots are lit on both ramps and both circuits, on the same ladder as above.
- After every third jackpot the bay doors drop and the Hangar is lit for the super jackpot: 500,000.
- For the first 15 seconds a ball that drains is launched again.
- If the turbo meter fills with fewer than three balls in play, another ball is launched.
- It ends when one ball is left. The doors come back up.

Later multiballs in the same game need the doors opened for every Hangar shot. Locked balls stay locked when a ball drains and are worth a bonus; the bays are emptied when the game is over. Nothing locks while a multiball is running.

Once all three doors have dropped, they stay down until the rules raise them: after a lock that does not keep them open, when an Academy multiball ends, when the super jackpot is collected, when HANGAR SAFETY starts or is passed, and when Graduation Day starts or ends. A Hangar shot that locks nothing (during a Turbo multiball, say) leaves them down, and so does the end of a Turbo multiball, so the lock is still lit for the next shot.

A course can run during a multiball and a multiball can start during a course: shots count for both.

## Combos

Flow shots are the two circuits, the two ramps and the Turbine lane. A flow shot made within 4 seconds of a different flow shot is a combo: the second shot of a chain pays 20,000 on top, the third 40,000, the fourth 80,000 and the fifth 150,000 (`COMBO 2X` to `COMBO 5X`). The chain ends at five; the next shot starts a new one. Repeating the same shot does not extend the chain, but it does not break it.

The first five-shot chain of a game lights the extra ball.

## Overdrive

Light all three roll call lanes, then shoot the Turbine lane: `OVERDRIVE 2X` doubles nearly every award on the table for 20 seconds. Complete the roll call lanes again while it runs for 3X and a fresh 20 seconds. It ends with its time or with the ball.

## Extra credit and extra balls

- Extra credit: light both turbo targets, then shoot the Briefing room. One of: 25,000, 50,000, kickback relit, bonus multiplier +1, 10 seconds of ball saver, turbo meter +15, overdrive lit (never while overdrive is lit or running: then it is one of the others). The display says which.
- Extra ball: lit at the Briefing room by the third course passed, by the first five-shot combo chain, and by starting a second Academy multiball. At most two are given in a game and one can be held at a time; an extra ball that cannot be given pays 100,000 instead. `SHOOT AGAIN` after the ball ends.

## Saves

- Ball saver: a ball that drains within its first 15 seconds of play is given back, as often as it happens (`BALL SAVED`). The 15 seconds start when the ball leaves the launch lane and stand still while it is held in the Briefing room, the Hangar or on a ramp. The shoot-again lamp blinks while it runs, quickly for its last 3 seconds.
- A ball that drains within 2.5 seconds of being returned by the Briefing room or the Hangar is given back.
- Kickback: lit at the start of the game. It returns one ball from the left outlane and goes out. All four W-I-N-G letters relight it; with it already lit they raise the bonus multiplier instead.

## End-of-ball bonus

Counted up when the ball ends:

| For | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Each course passed so far this game | 20,000 |
| Each ball locked in the Hangar | 10,000 |
| Each combo this ball | 5,000 |
| Each set of lanes completed this ball (roll call, W-I-N-G) | 5,000 |
| Each switch hit this ball, up to 400 | 250 |

The sum is multiplied by the bonus multiplier, 1X to 5X (from W-I-N-G with the kickback lit, or from extra credit). The multiplier starts again at 1X with each ball.

## Graduation Day

Pass all six courses in one game and Graduation Day is lit at the Briefing room.

1. Shoot the Briefing room: `GRADUATION DAY`. Every ball there is comes into play, three in all, and for 30 seconds a ball that drains is launched again. Nothing locks, no course runs and a full turbo meter pays the turbo bonus instead of a ball; combos, overdrive and the turbo meter go on.
2. The final exam: all seven major shots are lit (both circuits, both ramps, the Hangar, the Briefing room, the Turbine). Each pays 100,000 and goes out.
3. Commencement: with all seven made, the Hangar alone is lit: 1,000,000, and the rank of CHANCELLOR.
4. Victory laps: every major shot pays 50,000 until one ball is left.

If it ends before commencement, the exam shots you made stay made and three ramp shots light Graduation Day again. After a completed Graduation Day all six courses open again and each pass award is doubled; the rank stays.

The score stops at 999,999,999,999.

## Nudge and tilt

X bumps the table from the left and sends the ball right, `.` does the opposite, and Up bumps from the bottom and sends the ball up the table. The drawn table jolts and settles.

Each nudge adds tilt heat, which cools over 2.5 seconds. Two quick nudges show a warning. Three inside about a second tilt the table: the flippers and plunger go dead, nothing scores, no ball is given back, the end-of-ball bonus is lost and a running course is failed, until the last ball on the table has drained. A ball waiting on the plunger is launched by the table so that the tilt always ends. Locked balls stay locked. The next ball starts clean.

The rules live in `src/core/Rules` and `src/core/Courses` with no dependency on physics, and are unit tested event by event.
